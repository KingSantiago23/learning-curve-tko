Method and limits

Cumulative events are counted from the first row (Q1 2024), not from UFC's first event, so b can be biased. Cost is the UFC segment measure of direct operating costs (not a GAAP line, and not COGS by name); Q4 values are derived as full year minus nine months. Units are direct event counts, but a numbered event and a Fight Night cost very different amounts, so cost per event is lumpy. Q2 2026 includes UFC Freedom 250 (White House), a one-off. Costs are nominal dollars.
